OpenMatch was a job platform I built for a talent-graph company: a two-sided product connecting candidates and recruiters, with AI built into the resume and matching experience.

## The problem

Two-sided platforms have to be useful to both sides from day one. Candidates need a reason to build a profile, and recruiters need enough signal to act on it. The client wanted resume tooling strong enough to attract candidates on its own, and matching good enough to make the recruiter side worth paying for.

## What I built

- **For candidates:** AI-assisted resume building, including a quiz-based flow for people who don't know where to start, ATS-friendly templates, resume scoring and job matching.
- **For recruiters:** job posting, candidate matching and recruiter tooling.
- **Platform:** subscription billing with Stripe, analytics dashboards with ApexCharts, and a rich interface built on Nuxt and Vuetify with Vuex for state management.

## The stack

Nuxt and Vue.js with Vuex, Vuetify for the component system, Stripe for subscriptions and ApexCharts for dashboards.

## Status

OpenMatch is no longer online. It's included here because it shows a complete two-sided product with billing, built to a client's specification.
